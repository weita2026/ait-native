# AIT vs Git: 200-admitted-session game-development benchmark

This result accompanies AIT Native 1.1.0. It measures GPT-5.6 Sol at max reasoning effort on five frozen game-development workloads, with 20 statistically admitted paired attempts per workload and one fresh model session per lane.

## Result

- Workload-median token saving: **36.28%**.
- Aggregate bootstrap 95% confidence interval: **28.26% to 41.83%**.
- Workload-median elapsed-time saving: **15.22%**.
- Raw provider-total tokens: **45,432,262 AIT** versus **71,238,660 Git**, a descriptive **36.23%** reduction.
- Effective functional acceptance: **100/100 AIT** versus **100/100 Git**; all 200 statistically admitted sessions were protocol-valid and accepted.
- Evidence history: **203 executed sessions**; **3 session(s) were statistically excluded**.
- Source-protocol claim eligible: **false**.
- Current policy: `game-development-2026-08-29.36` (`owner_authorized_recovery_adjudication_and_statistical_replacement` evaluation); criteria met: **true**.
- Effective recovery-policy-qualified claim eligible: **true**.

The primary metric divides each workload and mode's statistically admitted provider tokens by accepted outcomes, then takes the median of the five workload-level AIT savings. The bootstrap interval resamples admitted paired attempts within each workload and aggregates their workload medians over 2,000 deterministic resamples. Raw pooled totals are descriptive and do not replace the workload-balanced primary metric.

The measured AIT subject was source Snapshot `SNP-46C179E4EE2D` with executable SHA-256 `722843d8355a86c6148ce2d520be202a4775a2a558911183d4611b68273c78e9`. The campaign runner SHA-256 was `ff1b4b8903c80fb46f24f768bcfdf8ab71f0d1839d75f2c1dc799bfb615db43a`. AIT Native 1.1.0 publishes this immutable result and the corrected report boundary without relabeling later source bytes as the measured subject.
